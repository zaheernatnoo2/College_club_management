FAQs

Everything you need to know before you join.

Who can join Nexus Clubs?+
Any enrolled student regardless of year or department can apply to join any of our clubs.
Is there a membership fee?+
No. All Nexus Clubs are completely free to join. We believe opportunity shouldn't have a price tag.
Can I join multiple clubs?+
Yes! Many of our members are active in 2–3 clubs. We encourage cross-club collaboration.
When are meetings held?+
Each club meets weekly, typically on weekday evenings to fit around class schedules.
